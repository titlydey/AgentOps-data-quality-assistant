Data quality has five core dimensions: completeness (no missing values), uniqueness (no duplicate records), validity (values match the expected format, such as valid dates), consistency (the same fact agrees across sources) and timeliness (data is fresh enough to use).

Anomaly detection finds values that break the usual pattern. Isolation Forest isolates outliers by random splits and works well on numeric columns without labels. Always review flagged rows manually before deleting them.

AgentOps means monitoring and testing LLM agents in production: log every tool call, evaluate routing accuracy on a fixed test set, and track failures.

Responsible AI guardrails for agents: block prompt-injection attempts, redact personal data such as emails and phone numbers from outputs, limit input length and only call approved tools.
